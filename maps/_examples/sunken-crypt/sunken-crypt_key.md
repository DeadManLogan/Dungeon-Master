# The Sunken Crypt — DM key

*Source: Example map (original)*

Grid: 36 × 26 squares, 1 square = 5 ft.

## 1. Entry Hall
Stairs descend from the ruined chapel above. Dust shows recent footprints heading east.

## 2. Hall of Pillars
Eight pillars hold up a vaulted ceiling. Two skeletal guardians wait behind the statues (A).

## 3. Chapel of the Drowned God
An altar to a sea deity. The candles never go out.

## 4. Burial Vault
Three sarcophagi. The middle one holds the false abbot.

## 5. Storeroom
Spoiled supplies. A partition wall splits off a small office.

## 6. Flooded Grotto
Black water fills the centre of the cave. Something large moves beneath it.

## 7. Abbot's Hoard
*Hidden area — not on the player handout.*
Secret treasury reached from the Entry Hall. Poison-needle trap on the chest.

## Doors, traps & markers

- **locked** door at (17,21)–(19,21): Key carried by the false abbot
- **secret** door at (4,10)–(5,10): Push the third brick; DC 15 Perception to notice
- **trap** T1 at (10.5,13.5): Pressure plate: 2d6 darts
- **marker** A at (17,11)
- **marker** A at (19,11)
- **trap** T2 at (5,3.9): Poison needle on the chest lock
