# The Delian Tomb — DM key

*Source: The Delian Tomb, map p.6, rooms p.7-8*

Grid: 13 × 15 squares, 1 square = 5 ft.

## A. The Offering Room (p. 7)
Five goblins cooking and dicing (if alerted they flip tables and take cover with bows). Two copper bowls: one is their campfire, one their toilet (scroll of Magic Missile under it, DC 15 Perception). Walls carved with the Delian knights and the Delian Oath — the oath is the answer to the riddle in B. The goblin patrol attacks from behind one round into this fight if it wasn't met outside.

## B. The Prayer Room (p. 7)
Pale stone, niches for Delian paladins, statue of the order's founder on a dais. Peter is in a crude iron cage (padlock: smash it). Five goblins + a goblin priest mid-ritual (disadvantage on Perception until someone attacks or enters); a bugbear in the corner by the door, unseen from the hall. All fight to the death; they offer to spare a downed PC if the party leaves Peter. Riddle on the dais (Elvish): "To keep this, you must first give it to me." — answer: your word. Swearing the Delian Oath opens the secret door in the back corner.

## C. The Tomb (p. 8)
*Hidden area — not on the player handout.*
Holy place. The Edict Blade (+1 longsword, +2 vs goblinoids) on a weapon stand. Oath-sworn PCs know they may take the sword and nothing else. If an unsworn PC takes it, or anyone opens a sarcophagus or takes anything else, 3 skeletons rise from random sarcophagi. Each sarcophagus: 3d20 gp of stones/offerings; each skeleton wears a 30 gp gold circlet; tapestry 45 gp.
