# Campaign index — The Delian Tomb

Source: The_Delian_Tomb.pdf (this folder) · extracted: extracted/ · maps: [maps/the-delian-tomb](../../maps/the-delian-tomb/) · 9 pages

A 4-hour one-shot for 1st–3rd level characters. Adapted by Seth Harris from Matt
Colville's concept. Generic setting. No PDF bookmarks; the sections below come from
the page headings.

## Other assets spotted (for later)

- **Magic items:**
  - Lucky Stone, Assassin's Belt (p.9; offered on p.3–4)
  - The Edict Blade, +1 longsword, +2 vs goblinoids (p.8)
  - scroll of Magic Missile (p.7)
  - 2 Potions of Healing sold at 25 gp (p.4)
- **Treasure:** sarcophagi 3d20 gp each, gold circlets 30 gp per skeleton, tapestry 45 gp (p.8).
- **NPCs:**
  - Thiago, caravan master (p.2, 4)
  - Elias Mabbett, patriarch (p.3)
  - Maja, 11 (p.3–4)
  - Peter Mabbett, the kidnapped child (p.8)
  - the Treharne family (p.3)
- **Monsters:**
  - goblins: patrol 2, guards 2, Offering Room 5, Prayer Room 5
  - goblin priest, bugbear (p.7)
  - 3 skeletons (p.8)
  - 2 wild boars (random, p.5)
- **Traps and puzzles:**
  - Scythe trap: DC 15 to spot, +8, 1d4 (p.7).
  - Oath riddle on the dais: "To keep this, you must first give it to me." Answer: your word (p.8).
